# Initial infrastructure implementation

## Intention

Implement the approved [AiNetReview concept](../initial-infrastructure/AiNetReview-Konzept.md) through a sequence of independently verifiable changes. The linked concept and its four epics are the sole product contracts; this document does not redefine them.

## Scope

### Must

- Follow the [approved concept](../initial-infrastructure/AiNetReview-Konzept.md) and [Epic 1](../initial-infrastructure/epics/01-Eingaben-und-Host.md), [Epic 2](../initial-infrastructure/epics/02-Regel-und-Findings.md), [Epic 3](../initial-infrastructure/epics/03-Berichte.md), and [Epic 4](../initial-infrastructure/epics/04-Umsetzung-und-Abnahme.md).
- Use [the roadmap](roadmap.md) to order and verify the implementation. Begin with the independently verified cleanup required by Epic 4.
- Meet the definition of done in Epic 4, including its specified test levels and current-state documentation updates.

### Not included

- Product behavior beyond the approved concept and its four epics.
- Implementation work in this concept and roadmap preparation task.

## Verification

The implementation is complete only when the acceptance criteria and definition of done in the linked epics are verified and every roadmap item, including the final audit, is closed. This planning task is complete when this concept and its roadmap are internally consistent and reference the approved contracts without duplicating them.
